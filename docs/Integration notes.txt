Athletics WA Records Management System

The Athletics WA Records Management System provides a central, secure platform for managing State, Championship and Association records across all athletics disciplines and age groups.

Staff can search, view, create, edit and archive records while maintaining a complete audit trail of all changes. Records are organised by event, age group, gender and record type, ensuring consistent governance and accurate record keeping.

When a new record is entered, the system automatically checks whether a current record already exists for that category. If a record is being broken, the previous record is automatically superseded and retained as part of the historical record. This removes the risk of accidentally creating duplicate current records and ensures record lineage is maintained.

The system also supports athlete management. If an athlete is not already in the database, staff can create a new athlete directly from the record creation workflow without losing any data already entered. This streamlines data entry and improves efficiency.

Additional features include user administration, approval workflows, audit logs, reporting, and bulk data import tools. These capabilities assist administrators in maintaining data quality, tracking changes, and managing records across the organisation.

The overall goal of the platform is to provide Athletics WA with a modern, reliable and auditable records management solution that preserves historical records, supports governance requirements, and simplifies the administration of athletics achievements across all disciplines and age groups.
