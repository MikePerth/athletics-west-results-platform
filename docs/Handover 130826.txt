Athletics West Records Platform - Handover Document
Executive Summary

The Athletics West Records Platform is a web-based records management system designed to manage, display, audit, approve and maintain athletics records across multiple athletics schemes.

The platform currently supports:

Athletics WA (Senior Athletics)
Little Athletics WA (Junior Athletics)

The application provides:

Public records display
Athlete management
Record management
Record creation and editing
Record approval workflows
Evidence management
Audit logging
Data import facilities
Historical record tracking
Frozen/permanent records

Audit logging
Approval workflows

Historical tracking
Frozen/permanent records

Public-facing Little Athletics display enhancements.
Ongoing records administration and maintenance.
